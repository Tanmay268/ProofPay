ProofPay local demo, no real funds

Pay an invoice from a shielded pool, then prove it was paid without revealing who paid. All four roles run on your machine.

1. Merchant

Creates invoices. Each payment lands on a fresh one-time (stealth) address.

2. Payer

Deposits into the pool, then pays invoices with a zero-knowledge proof.

3. Third-party verifier

E.g. a delivery service. Accepts a receipt once; learns nothing about the payer.

4. Compliance

Deposit screening and per-payment disclosure to an auditor.

What a public blockchain observer sees

Note the payment's sender is the relayer (or you, if you pay directly), the recipient is a one-time address, and nothing links a deposit to a payment.
